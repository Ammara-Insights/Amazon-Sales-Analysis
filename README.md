# Amazon Product & Pricing Analytics
 <h1>Project Overview</h1>

Amazon offers a wide range of products with different prices, discount levels, ratings, and customer engagement. This project analyzes Amazon product data to understand how pricing and discounts relate to customer ratings, reviews, and savings.

The analysis focuses on identifying category-level patterns and converting raw product data into meaningful business insights that can support better pricing and product decisions.

## Tools Used

* Microsoft Power BI
* Power Query
* DAX
* Data Cleaning
* Data Visualization
* Dashboard Design


## <b>Objectives</b>

* Analyze product pricing and discount patterns
* Understand the relationship between discounts and customer ratings
* Compare customer ratings across product categories
* Analyze customer savings across categories
* Examine customer reviews and engagement
* Identify meaningful business insights from the data

## <b>Key Insights</b>

* Higher discounts do not consistently correspond to higher customer ratings.
* Customer ratings vary across product categories.
* Electronics provides the highest average customer saving per product, followed by Home & Kitchen.
* Higher customer savings are not determined by discount percentage alone.
